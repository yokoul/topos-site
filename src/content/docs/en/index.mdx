    <span class="tp-label">Getting started</span>
    <h2>Four steps, a laser meter, one afternoon.</h2>
    <p>Everything you need is on this site: the files to print, the firmware, the guide. Nothing proprietary, nothing to buy that isn't online.</p>
    <a class="sl-link-button primary" href="/en/start/01-hardware/">Read the guide</a>
  </div>
  <ol class="tp-steps">
    <li><div><b>Print and assemble three anchors</b><span>STL provided, MaUWB ESP32-S3 module, base + snap-on lid.</span></div><small>printing</small></li>
    <li><div><b>Flash and join the WiFi</b><span>Captive portal on first power-up. No reflash per venue.</span></div><small>10 min</small></li>
    <li><div><b>Measure with the laser meter, enter in millimetres</b><span>Topos derives anchor positions by trilateration.</span></div><small>15 min</small></li>
    <li><div><b>Connect the console</b><span>PSN to Soma or Augment3d, OSC to QLab, Art-Net to moving heads.</span></div><small>5 min</small></li>
  </ol>
