## A project built with venues

Topos runs in one theatre. It will get better with other stages, other consoles, other anchor geometries than ours. The most useful contribution isn't code: it's telling us what happened in your venue.

## Contribute

Docs, cases, code — everything is open. The contributing guide explains how to work without hardware (`--simulate`), what CI checks, and where to stop before touching module boundaries or protocols.
