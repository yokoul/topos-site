## Three anchors, one tag, a printer.

Everything is sold off the shelf by [Makerfabs](https://www.makerfabs.com). The cases are parametric OpenSCAD: if your module changes, the case follows.

<div class="tp-bom">

| Component | Role | Quantity | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| [MaUWB ESP32S3](https://www.makerfabs.com/mauwb-esp32s3-uwb-module.html) (DW3000 + PA) | Anchor (fixed, around the stage) | 3 minimum, 8 maximum | Makerfabs |
| [MaUWB Direct](https://www.makerfabs.com/mauwb-direct.html) | Tag (worn by the performer), or extra anchor | 1 per performer | Makerfabs |
| USB-C cable | Data, not charge-only | 1 per anchor | — |
| USB power supply, 5 V 1 A minimum | Anchors | depends on cabling | — |
| PLA or PETG filament | Cases | a few tens of grams per case | — |
| Laser distance meter | Venue calibration, millimetre precision | 1 | you already own one |

</div>

A minimal kit — three anchors in a triangle, never in a line, mounted at 2–3 m, one tag — is enough for 2D tracking on a theatre stage. Add anchors to cover more or go 3D. Details in the guide: [Required hardware](/en/start/01-hardware/).

## Printable cases

Two parts, base and snap-on lid, FDM **with no supports**. Designed for the MaUWB ESP32-S3 module (PCB 73 × 33 × 12 mm). OpenSCAD sources and STL in the repository, under CERN-OHL-P v2.

## Anchor firmware

Arduino for ESP32-S3: UWB ranging, WiFi via captive portal (no reflash per venue), UDP streaming to Topos, OLED showing the anchor ID and IP. One parameter to change per anchor before flashing: its number.

<a class="sl-link-button secondary" href="/en/reference/02-firmware/">Firmware guide</a>

## What Topos does not ask of you

No board to fabricate, no component to solder. Modules arrive ready to flash; tags are configured from the Topos dashboard, no Arduino IDE. A 3D printer for the cases, and that's it.
