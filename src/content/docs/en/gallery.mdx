## The dashboard

Captured in `--simulate` mode: three anchors, three performers, no hardware. This is exactly what you get after `python -m topos.main --simulate circle`.

<div class="tp-gallery">
  <Shot name="dashboard-show" alt="Topos dashboard in show mode: stage view, three anchors as diamonds, three named performers with their trail" title="Show">the operator's view. The stage from above, downstage at the bottom, upstage at the top. Each performer has a name, a position in metres, a signal quality and a ghost timeout.</Shot>
  <Shot name="dashboard-anchors" alt="Topos dashboard in anchors mode: calibration panel with heights and distances in millimetres, preset list" title="Anchors">calibration. Height and distances of each anchor in millimetres, as read on the laser meter; Topos computes the positions. Presets save a whole venue.</Shot>
